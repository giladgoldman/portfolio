Construction people who got into technology, not the other way round.

I started on the estimating and quantity surveying side of tier-one commercial construction, then moved into the roles that remove the friction slowing builders down: safety and compliance, financial process, procurement, and the systems that let a business scale without adding overhead.

I’m a pragmatic innovator who tests assumptions constantly. I can be decisive when pressure is high and quick to pivot when the facts change. I build trust with site teams, technical teams and executive boards by taking human needs and operational friction equally seriously.

My focus is simple: elevating legacy thinking into intuitive, scalable systems.
