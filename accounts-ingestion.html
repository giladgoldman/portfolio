Your accounts inbox is your source of truth. This tool reads it continuously, separates what’s unique from what’s duplicated, and shows what’s been paid and what’s still owed. It also shows what your other systems should say. It sends only the exceptions to a person, and what was actually paid goes into a rate library that prices your next job.

02 What it does

From a busy inbox to a short review list.

  1. Reads the inboxChecks every couple of minutes, filters spam, opens forwarded emails and reads PDFs, Word files and scans.
  2. Sorts and extractsInvoice, statement, progress claim, credit note, remittance and more, each with vendor, references, project, amounts and dates.
  3. Flags, never guessesMissing PO, no project, duplicates, overdue items and changed bank details go to a person. Bank changes always get a phone check.
  4. Drafts, never sendsWhere a reply is needed it writes one for review. Nothing goes out without a human.
  5. Learns from correctionsEach week the fixes people made become updated rules, switched on only if they score better on a test set.

A person reads the invoice and confirms the real per-unit rate. The first rate set for an item locks its unit, so one vendor’s weekly price never gets blended with another’s daily one.

05 Why it matters

Price from what you actually paid, not what the schedule says.

  • EstimatingDefensible preliminaries rates with ranges, not one estimator’s memory.
  • FinanceHours of opening, reading and re-keying become a short review.
  • RiskDuplicates, overdue items and bank-detail changes are caught before they are paid.
06 How it’s built

Plain, dependable plumbing, with a person at every decision.

  • ServiceA Node.js service watches the shared mailbox through the Microsoft Graph API every couple of minutes and writes each record to a database.
  • ExtractionText is read straight from PDFs and Word files, with OCR for scans. AI models pull out the fields, and the review screen shows the original beside the result.
  • Learning loopEach week, human corrections are distilled into updated extraction rules. They go live only if they score better against a fixed test set.
  • AIMultiple AI models sit behind one shared wrapper, with automatic failover, so one provider having a bad day doesn’t stop the tool.
  • HostingDeployed to Railway from GitHub. The review screen is a React app built on a shared component library, and automated probes check every endpoint.
  • SafeguardsRead-only toward accounting and project systems. Replies are drafted, never sent. Sign-off is recorded per attachment.
