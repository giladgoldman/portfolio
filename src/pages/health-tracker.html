02 What it does

Food in. Exercise out. One place.

  1. Meals in plain EnglishI describe what I ate. AI estimates the calories, protein, fat and fibre and logs it.
  2. Lifting, walks and ridesWeights, cardio walks and bike rides are logged next to the food, instead of a separate app for each.
  3. One daily balanceCalories in and out, plus protein, fibre and fat, side by side at a glance.
  4. Body and trendsWeight, BMI, muscle and fat, training programs, and a summary of averages and trends.
  5. Yours to keepIt lives in my own Notion workspace, not in a fitness app’s database. I can export it, reshape it or add to it whenever I like.

04 Why it’s here

Proof that I build things for fun too.

It isn’t a product and it isn’t a standalone app. Most trackers do one job, meals or weights or cardio, and keep the data in their own system. I wanted input and output together in one tool, and I wanted to own the data, so it lives in my own Notion. It’s here because it’s a good example of using AI to build something genuinely useful for one person, quickly, without a development team.

The same thinking sits behind the construction tools on this site.

05 How it’s built

Built entirely in Notion, with AI doing the tedious part.

  • PlatformNotion databases for meals, exercise, lifts and body measurements, with dashboards that roll the daily totals together.
  • AIA plain-English meal description is turned into estimated calories, protein, fat and fibre and logged automatically.
  • OwnershipThere is no server, app or subscription of mine in the middle. The data sits in my own workspace and I can export or restructure it any time.
  • PrivacyMy real health data is never read by this site. Everything shown here is invented sample data.
