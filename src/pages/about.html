03 Customer success

Seven years looking after customers.

Customer Success Manager, then Customer Success Lead, then Head of Customer Success, in software companies serving construction and beyond. The job is the whole relationship, from the first week to the renewal conversation and everything in between.

  1. OnboardingGetting new customers set up, using the product and seeing value early, so the first weeks build habits instead of doubts.
  2. TrainingTeaching customers and their teams how to get the most from the product, in the format that suits them: one to one, in groups, or self-serve.
  3. Relationship managementKnowing who matters on the customer side, keeping regular contact, and being the person they call when something needs sorting.
  4. Engagement trackingWatching how customers actually use the product, and spotting quiet accounts before they become silent ones.
  5. RenewalsPreparing for renewal from day one, so it is a conversation about results rather than a last-minute scramble.
  6. Churn mitigationReading the early warning signs, finding out what is really going wrong, and stepping in with a plan while there is still time.
