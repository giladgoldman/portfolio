02 What it does

From a busy inbox to a short review list.

  1. Reads the inboxChecks every couple of minutes, filters spam, opens forwarded emails and reads PDFs, Word files and scans.
  2. Sorts and extractsInvoice, statement, progress claim, credit note, remittance and more, each with vendor, references, project, amounts and dates.
  3. Flags, never guessesMissing PO, no project, duplicates, overdue items and changed bank details go to a person. Bank changes always get a phone check.
  4. Drafts, never sendsWhere a reply is needed it writes one for review. Nothing goes out without a human.
  5. Learns from correctionsEach week the fixes people made become updated rules, switched on only if they score better on a test set.

05 Why it matters

Price from what you actually paid, not what the schedule says.

  • EstimatingDefensible preliminaries rates with ranges, not one estimator’s memory.
  • FinanceHours of opening, reading and re-keying become a short review.
  • RiskDuplicates, overdue items and bank-detail changes are caught before they are paid.
