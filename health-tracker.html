02 What it does

Tell it what you ate. It does the maths.

  1. Meals in plain EnglishI describe what I ate. AI estimates the calories, protein, fat and fibre and logs it.
  2. Training and liftsSeparate logs for exercise and for lifting, so output can be tracked over time.
  3. Body trackingWeight, BMI and muscle and fat in one place.
  4. Daily dashboardsCalories in and out, plus protein, fibre and fat, at a glance.
  5. Programs and trendsTraining programs, and a summary of averages and trends.

04 Why it’s here

Proof that I build things for fun too.

It isn’t a product and it isn’t a standalone app. It lives in Notion. It’s here because it’s a good example of using AI to build something genuinely useful for one person, quickly, without a development team.
