Construction people who got into technology, not the other way round.

I started on the estimating and quantity surveying side of tier-one commercial construction, then moved into the roles that remove the friction slowing builders down: safety and compliance, financial process, procurement, and the systems that let a business scale without adding overhead.

I’m a pragmatic innovator who tests assumptions constantly. I can be decisive when pressure is high and quick to pivot when the facts change. I build trust with site teams, technical teams and executive boards by taking human needs and operational friction equally seriously.

My focus is simple: elevating legacy thinking into intuitive, scalable systems.

02 What it does

From a pile of PDFs to a pricing asset.

  1. Ingest anythingInvoices, progress claims and statements by upload or inbox, in whatever format suppliers send.
  2. Extract with receiptsEvery line gets quantity, unit, rate and total, with the source location kept so any figure can be traced back.
  3. Code to your structureLines map to your cost codes. Low confidence goes to a person, never silently guessed, and corrections teach it.
  4. Check the mathsLine items are reconciled against invoice totals so errors surface before they reach the ledger.

02 What it does

Inputs in. Defensible method out.

  1. Describe the jobScope, drawings, site constraints, programme windows and the standards that apply.
  2. Sequence itActivities are ordered with dependencies, hold points and resourcing.
  3. Challenge itRisks and controls sit next to each step so experienced people can push back fast.
  4. Issue itExport in your template, and reuse as the starting point for the next similar job.
03 Principles

Built to support experienced judgement, not replace it.

  • Visible reasoningEvery step shows why it’s there and what it depends on.
  • Human sign-offThe tool drafts and structures. Qualified people review and approve.
  • Reusable know-howCapture how your best people build so each job doesn’t start from a blank page.
