02 What it does

Upload, draft, export.

  1. Upload the packDrop in the tender zip. Every file is listed with what it is and whether it was read. Prices and contract sums are left out on purpose, and the files are deleted once read.
  2. Draft as a documentApproved wording, this job’s facts and written passages come together. Click any phrase to see its source, pick another option or type your own.
  3. Check itThe draft is matched against the tender’s requirements and checked for slips before anyone relies on it.
  4. Export to WordWith highlighted flags and review notes for the team, or a clean version. A single editor at a time keeps the draft from being overwritten.

04 Principles

Built to support experienced judgement, not replace it.

  • Sources on every factEach fact carries the tender words it came from. Facts it can’t verify against the tender text are dropped.
  • Nothing swapped silentlyWhere the tender disagrees with itself or the draft, both options are shown and a person chooses.
  • Human sign-offIt drafts and flags. Qualified people review, edit and approve, and score each section so the tool improves.
05 How it’s built

A document editor on the outside, a pipeline of checks underneath.

  • ServiceA Node.js and Express service backed by Postgres. Each tender is a saved run, and drafting happens in background jobs so you can keep working.
  • Facts firstAI reads facts from the tender text. Each carries the words it came from, and anything that can’t be verified against the text is dropped.
  • Wording libraryApproved wording lives in a maintained library of reusable blocks, so changes are made once and flow into every new draft.
  • EditingChanges save as you go with undo and history, and a one-editor lock stops two people overwriting each other.
  • ExportWord files are generated from templates with docxtemplater, with flags and review notes highlighted.
  • ResilienceUploaded files are deleted once read. If the free AI quota runs out, reading stops with a clear message and resumes later, and finished work is kept.
