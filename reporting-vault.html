Reporting
Vault

Every month and every week the business produces a report, and the numbers inside it are filed away and forgotten. The Vault keeps them. It stores the figures from each regularly generated report, fills in the history from the tools in the tech stack, and turns the lot into charts you can ask for in plain words.

02 What it does

Keep the numbers. Chart them later.

  1. Keeps every reportMonthly safety reports and weekly project dashboards are uploaded once. The figures are pulled out and stored, and the original file is kept alongside them.
  2. Consolidates the stackEach night it reads the tools the business already uses, read-only, and copies across only counts, dates and project names: hours worked, incidents, permits, inductions and safety checks.
  3. Fills in the pastHistory that was never saved from a report is rebuilt from the tools, going back years, so trend lines start long before the Vault did.
  4. Charts on requestAsk for a chart in words, such as incidents by project over the last year, and it draws it.
  5. Answers questionsAsk what changed between two months and get a short answer based on the stored figures.

04 Why it’s here

Reports are a snapshot. The value is in the trend.

A monthly report answers what happened this month. It can’t say whether things are getting better, because last year’s numbers are in a different file, or in a different tool, or nowhere. A reporting product could fix that, for a subscription. The Vault does the one job that matters: keep the figures from the reports the business already produces, so they can be charted over time.

It sits alongside the automated checks that confirm each report was filed.

05 Principles

Keep the figures. Never keep the people.

  • Counts, not peopleOnly counts, dates and project names are copied from other tools. Names, birth dates, phone numbers and descriptions of the people involved are never copied.
  • Read-onlyThe Vault reads from the tools in the stack and can’t change anything in them.
  • Reports winFigures from other tools are labelled as such and never replace a saved report.
  • Nothing deletedA wrong report is withdrawn, not erased, so the history stays honest.
  • Figures stay homeWhen AI draws a chart it sees metric names, project names and months, not the figures. The text of incidents is never sent anywhere.
  • Admin onlyAccess is limited to the administrator, and enforced on the server.
06 How it’s built

A small private database, a nightly sync, and AI only where it earns its place.

  • StorageExtracted figures and the original report file sit together in the business’s own private database, so every number can be traced back to its source document.
  • SyncA nightly job reads the other tools through read-only connections and stores only the allowed fields, each labelled with where it came from.
  • ChartsAI turns a plain-English request into a chart definition using metric, project and month names only. The chart itself is drawn from stored figures.
  • AnswersFor a question, the AI sees the question and only the few figures or list lines needed to answer it.
  • StatusBeing tested with the administrator before wider use.
